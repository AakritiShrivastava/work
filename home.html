Aakriti Shrivastava

Making world-altering solutions go farther.

The problems we face are big. But so are the solutions. My work is dedicated to amplifying those solutions — and, most importantly, the people and voices leading them.

In communications, strategy and stories are two sides of the same coin. Strategy helps us see connections and patterns at a macro level; storytelling makes them visible, tangible, and meaningful. I do both.

In a decade long career in communications, I have been turning complex ideas into communications that people can understand, care about and act on. Across journalism and the nonprofit sector, I’ve built campaigns, platforms, communities and narratives around issues I believe matter.
