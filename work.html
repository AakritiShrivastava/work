1 Lead, Digital Strategy and Messaging

Website Launch and Digital Platform Strategy

I led the launch of the Grassroots Justice Network’s website as part of a broader renaming and rebranding process. My work included developing the site’s core messaging and narrative architecture, shaping the visual brand in collaboration with designers, and structuring the site to support clear user journeys for members, partners, and prospective audiences.

The site was designed not only as an information hub, but as a platform for participation—connecting users to learning opportunities, community spaces, and collective action. The launch was supported by coordinated social media promotion and audience acquisition efforts.

2 Coordinator, Content Advisor, and Dissemination Lead

Podcast: A Common Pot

A Common Pot is an in-house narrative podcast exploring how grassroots justice movements build power and drive systems change across different contexts. I serve as the overall coordinator for the podcast, advising on editorial direction, shaping episode narratives, and leading dissemination across digital channels.

My role bridges content and strategy—ensuring each episode contributes to broader organisational priorities around learning, movement building, and global solidarity, while also reaching and engaging new audiences.

3 Lead, Communications and Programming

Global Virtual Event Series: Building Power Across Contexts

I led the design and delivery of a global virtual event series bringing together justice defenders from different regions to share strategies on building power, responding to repression, and strengthening community leadership.

My responsibilities included shaping the thematic framing, developing promotional messaging, coordinating speakers across time zones, and managing dissemination before and after each event. The series functioned both as a live convening space and as long-form digital content for ongoing learning and engagement.
